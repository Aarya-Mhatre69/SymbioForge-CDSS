Heart health check

A short guided check, powered by SymbioForge, that gives you a plain-language overview of what stands out in your heart health and what to do next. It takes about three minutes.

Not a diagnosis. If you have severe chest pain, pain spreading to the arm or jaw, breathlessness or fainting, call your local emergency number now instead of using this tool.

What happens

  1. A few simple questions. Your age, any chest discomfort, and a few check-up numbers. Every term is explained as you go.
  2. Your overview. What stands out, how sure the tool is, and which tests would help most. You can then book a home checkup or a clinic visit.

What helps to have ready

  • Your blood pressure and cholesterol numbers from a recent check-up. A home-visit technician can measure these for you.
  • Your resting ECG and treadmill stress-test results. If you do not have them, ask your doctor, or book a home checkup first.
  • Optional: results of specialised tests (thallium scan, vessel count). Most people have not had these, and that is fine.
