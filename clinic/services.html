Services

What we offer, and where each test is done.

Home checkup visit

A technician comes to you and collects your blood pressure, a resting ECG, a fasting blood sugar and a cholesterol panel at home.

Stress testing, nuclear scan and angiography are not done at home. They are done at the clinic only.

Book a home checkup

ECG and stress testing

Resting ECG and exercise stress testing. Stress testing is performed at the clinic only.

Cardiology consults

Talk through your results and options with a cardiologist. Nuclear scan and angiography, when needed, are arranged and done at the clinic only.

Family medicine

Routine and ongoing care for the whole family.

Heart health check

A free guided overview you can do from your sofa. It is not a diagnosis.
